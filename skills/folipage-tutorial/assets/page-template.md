---
title: Create an invoice
description: Issue an invoice to a customer and send it by e-mail.
order: 2
---

You create an invoice whenever you bill a customer for finished work. It takes
about two minutes once the customer exists.

:::note[Before you start]
You need the **Accountant** or **Admin** role, and the customer must already
be in **Customers**. See [Add a customer](add-a-customer.md).
:::

::::steps
:::step{title="Open the invoices"}
In the left menu, click **Invoices**. The list of your invoices opens.

![The main menu with Invoices highlighted.](images/create-an-invoice-1.png)
:::

:::step{title="Start a new invoice"}
In the top right corner, click **New invoice**.

![The invoice list with the New invoice button highlighted.](images/create-an-invoice-2.png)
:::

:::step{title="Fill in the invoice"}
Fill in the fields that matter:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Start typing the name and select the customer. |
| **Due date** | 14 days is the default; change it if you agreed otherwise. |
| **Items** | One line per product or service, with quantity and price. |

![The new invoice form with the Customer field highlighted.](images/create-an-invoice-3.png)
:::

:::step{title="Send the invoice"}
Click **Send**. Check the recipient in the dialog and click **Send invoice**.
:::
::::

:::warning
A sent invoice cannot be edited. Correct it with a credit note.
:::

The invoice now shows in the list with the status **Sent**.
